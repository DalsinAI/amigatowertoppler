Tower Toppler: licences
=======================

The game: Tower Toppler (Nebulus) 1.3 by Andreas Röver and Pasi
Kallinen, with graphics partly by Bastian Salmela, music by Angel Keha and
missions by Clarence Ball (AUTHORS), https://gitlab.com/roever/toppler,
commit c8bf02b0f8bf8875a454758227bf2bfc029487c3. A remake of Nebulus by
John M. Phillips (Hewson, 1987); it holds nothing of the original game.

- The program (src/) is under the GNU General Public License, version 2
  or (at your option) any later version, as each source file says; the
  licence text upstream ships is the GPL version 3 (COPYING.txt).
- toppler.dat (the graphics, sounds, fonts and the missions) is built from
  datafile/ in the same GPL source tree (upstream's Makefile and datafile/
  README make it; the prebuilt file is upstream's own, unchanged).
- toppler.ogg (the title music, Angel Keha) comes in the same tree, under
  the same GPL; upstream gives no other licence for any of its data, and
  Debian distributes the whole package, data and music included, in main
  (its debian/copyright gives the GPL, Copyright 2000-2022 Andreas Röver,
  and no other licence).
- The icons (amiga/icons/) are drawn from upstream's dist/toppler40.xpm
  and are under the same GPL.
- The Amiga port's own files (amiga/) are GPL 2 or later, like the game.
  amiga/amiga_screens.c and amiga/amiga_message.c come from the AmiCradle
  Team's Seven Kingdoms and Abbaye ports.

At run time the program uses GFOpenGPU's SDL2.module and SDL2_mixer.module
(SDL 2 and SDL_mixer: zlib licence) and is linked with zlib (zlib
licence); the modules are installed with GFOpenGPU and are not part of
this drawer.
